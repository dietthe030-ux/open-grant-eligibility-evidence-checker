# Stage 2 — Implementation Specification

Storage: owner/upgrader addresses, `TreeMap[Address,bool]` authorized publishers, immutable `TreeMap[str,GrantSpecification]`, and `TreeMap[str,Application]`. The deployer is the sole Root upgrader for bounded recovery. A specification binds publisher, canonical URL, expected evidence digest, criterion IDs, deadline and observation window. API: upgrader-only `upgrade(new_code)`; owner-only `set_publisher_authorization`; publisher-only `register_grant_specification`; applicant `create_application(application_id, grant_specification_id, region, org_type, submitted_at)` and `freeze_application(application_id)`; public assess/retry/views. Caller cannot supply `eligible`, URL, digest, criterion IDs, deadline, or observation bounds during the applicant journey. Leader/validator independently refetch, require exact canonical URL and the publisher-precommitted digest, derive the result, and compare every consequential field including digest. The source does not self-authorize by declaring an on-chain specification ID. WRONG: applicant-selected `grant_url` or `assess(case_id, eligible: bool)`. CORRECT: `assess(case_id)` derives only from the immutable publisher-bound snapshot and self-declared applicant facts. Test authorization, revocation, immutable IDs, unknown references, deadline boundaries, source/digest mismatches, missing criteria, source outage, disagreement and serialized return. Frontend must expose the trust boundary and actual transaction lifecycle.

Revision 2 schema adds `declared_facts_notice`, canonical `region_criterion_id`, `org_type_criterion_id`, `deadline_criterion_id`, `deadline_utc`, and observation bounds. Only exact enum membership and UTC comparison are allowed. Contradictory caller facts create separate conditional cases; UI/readback may never label them independently verified. Recheck ambiguous/contradictory source prose, missing IDs, before/on/after deadline, timezone variants, stale/unavailable source and disagreement. Version-recorded schema/equivalence/web/serialization probe is the first build-handoff gate.
